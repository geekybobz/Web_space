Mindset

My Philosophy

🧠

First Principle Thinking

I am most drawn to questions that force me to ask why before how. For me, theory is not something separate from computation; it is the structure that tells us what a system can do, what assumptions matter, and what kind of control is actually meaningful.

🌐

Curiosity Without Borders

I do not like staying rigidly inside one box. Physics, numerics, coding, optimization, and machine learning all sharpen different parts of how I think, and I enjoy exploring across them when it helps me grow or solve a problem better.

🛠️

Understanding Through Building

I learn best by building things for myself, whether that means simulations, small tools, or full numerical workflows. That is also why I admire industry: it asks the useful question of how theory can become something practical, usable, and genuinely valuable.
