PhD Candidate · Theoretical Physics
Institut Carnot de Bourgogne (ICB) · Dijon, France · 3-year doctoral fellowship

Working on quantum control theory at ICB Dijon. The central question is how fast, cheap, and robust a quantum gate can be made.

The work uses the Pontryagin Maximum Principle and GRAPE to design energy-efficient protocols with timing constraints and bounded work fluctuations. A separate thread extends this to open quantum systems, where noise and dissipation are part of the problem from the start.

BS-MS Integrated Physics · CPI 7.9 / 10
Admitted via JEE Advanced · IISER Berhampur, India

Five years of integrated physics covering quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, classical mechanics, electrodynamics, condensed matter, and mathematical methods.

For the MS thesis, joined Victor Mukherjee's group working on RL-based optimal control for quantum batteries:
· Objective: maximize ergotropy in spin-½ chain systems
· Approach: Soft Actor-Critic learning charging protocols from scratch
· Scope: single-qubit to multi-qubit (2–5 qubits), ordered and disordered dynamics
· Outcome: RL protocols outperform greedy strategies in relevant charging regimes

Awarded the Chanakya Fellowship (iHub-QTF, IISER Pune) in the final year for this work.
