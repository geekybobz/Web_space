Past & Present

How I Got Here

I completed my BS-MS in Physics at IISER Berhampur, where I developed a strong foundation in physics and a sustained interest in quantum mechanics and quantum information. During this period, I was awarded the Chanakya Fellowship, which supported my master's research and allowed me to engage more deeply with these areas.

For my master's thesis, I worked under the guidance of Victor Mukherjee and Paolo Andrea Erdman, on problems related to quantum batteries. This work led me toward broader questions in control, optimization, and the identification of physically meaningful performance measures. Over time, I also developed a growing interest in machine learning and reinforcement learning, not only for their practical utility, but for the deeper questions they raise about how algorithms learn, generalize, and make predictions.

I am currently a PhD candidate at ICB Dijon, working under the supervision of Dominique Sugny and Camille, with current research focused on optimal and robust quantum control. In particular, I am interested in control strategies that remain effective under realistic constraints, noise, and uncertainty, as well as in the numerical methods required to make such approaches practically relevant.

Future Directions

Where I Want To Go

I want to keep pushing deeper into robust and numerically efficient quantum control, but I am equally interested in the broader space around it: open quantum systems, simulation-heavy workflows, machine-learning-assisted control, and theory that can survive contact with implementation.

I am also interested in problems where abstract physics can be translated into methods, software, or workflows that are genuinely useful beyond a single paper. This includes learning from adjacent areas and building the technical range needed to move between theory, numerics, and practical research tools.

This section is still evolving, but the long-term direction is clear: develop stronger control methods, stronger computational tools, and stronger intuition for how deep theory becomes practical value.
