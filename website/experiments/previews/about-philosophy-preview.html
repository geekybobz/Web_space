Who I Am

I completed my BS-MS in Physics at IISER Berhampur, supported by the Chanakya Fellowship. My master's thesis, supervised by Victor Mukherjee and Paolo Andrea Erdman, focused on quantum batteries via reinforcement learning, which drew me toward control, optimization, and the deeper questions around how algorithms learn and generalize.

I am now a PhD candidate at ICB Dijon, working with Dominique Sugny and Camille on optimal and robust quantum control. The long-term aim is control methods and computational tools that hold up under noise, realistic constraints, and contact with implementation.
