Quantum Battery Charging

Developed RL-based optimal control protocols for quantum batteries, targeting maximum ergotropy in spin-1/2 chain systems. Validated on a single-qubit system and extended to multi-qubit configurations (2 to 5 qubits) under both ordered and disordered dynamics, showing RL-guided control outperforms greedy protocols.

02

Quantum Control and Open Dynamics

Using analytical tools, particularly the Pontryagin Maximum Principle, and numerical methods such as GRAPE to design control protocols that minimize thermodynamic costs, work fluctuations, and control time. A second thread addresses open quantum systems where noise and dissipation make robust, energetically efficient control both harder and more relevant.
