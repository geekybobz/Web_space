Reading Model

This page has three depth levels. That is the core mechanism for keeping the story rich without overwhelming users.

Level 1 Main spine only: School, College, IISER, PhD.
Level 2 Selected chapters reveal compact sub-branches like MS Thesis, Posters, and Publications.
Level 3 Each branch item can later open its own detail page or container.
Image Strategy One anchor image for a major chapter. Small thumbnails for sub-items. Avoid large images everywhere.
