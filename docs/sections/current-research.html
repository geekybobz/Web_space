What I am working on now

My current work is centered on robust quantum control, with an emphasis on numerical optimization, realistic constraints, and control strategies that remain useful in noisy settings.

I am especially interested in problems where theoretical structure, computation, and implementation constraints all have to be handled together rather than in isolation.
